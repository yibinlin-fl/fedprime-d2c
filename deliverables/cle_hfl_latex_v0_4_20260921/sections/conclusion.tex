\section{Conclusion}

We study class--corruption entanglement in model-heterogeneous federated learning, where client-specific class--operator associations can turn corruption into directional class cues. Source-paired operator interventions and Directional Shortcut Alignment provide a target-aligned diagnostic under explicit identification assumptions, while proxy non-identifiability explains why pseudo-environment improvements alone cannot certify reduced CLE harm. A seed-0 matched HFL-versus-Local analysis indicates predominantly local-first formation in the controlled setting, motivating a taxonomy-assisted local intervention that combines coarse family-level PEW with BER. Completed experiments show substantial pooled DSA reductions under matched controls, on two controlled binding maps, and under a second protocol-matched HFL communication base, while also exposing mixed utility and taxonomy boundaries. The current claims remain limited by training-seed, single-private-dataset, and controlled-corruption scope.
