\section{Conclusion}

We study class--corruption entanglement in model-heterogeneous federated learning, where client-specific class--operator associations can make corruption a directional class cue. Source-paired operator interventions and Directional Shortcut Alignment measure this binding-directed response under explicit identification assumptions, while proxy non-identifiability shows why pseudo-environment improvements alone cannot certify reduced CLE dependence. The seed-0 factorial comparing HFL and Local training finds a predominantly local-first pattern, motivating a taxonomy-assisted intervention based on coarse family-level PEW and BER. On held-out map2, the four-arm comparison reproduces across training seeds 0/1/2: PEW+BER has lower pooled DSA and higher pooled task utility than ERM, CVaR-DRO, and PEW+GroupDRO. Seed-0 experiments also reproduce DSA suppression on another binding map and under FedDF-fidelity, where the utility gate fails. The remaining evidence gaps concern local-first training-seed stability, a second private dataset, and bounded taxonomy mismatch.
