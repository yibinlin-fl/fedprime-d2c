\section{Conclusion}

Class--corruption entanglement turns a nominal nuisance into a client-specific class cue in model-heterogeneous federated learning. Source-paired operator interventions and DSA make this response measurable without treating corrupted accuracy as shortcut evidence. The accompanying identification analysis clarifies both what the paired contrast removes and why pseudo-environment improvements cannot certify mitigation on their own. In the evaluated factorial, CLE is predominantly local-first, which motivates changing the client risk rather than introducing another communication protocol. PEW+BER implements that intervention with an explicit corruption taxonomy and class-conditional support balancing. Completed controlled studies show substantial DSA reductions across bindings and communication bases; the moderate protocol additionally shows a monotone seed-0 dose response and a 51.21\% matched DSA reduction, together with the important qualification that utility is not uniformly preserved. Remaining evidence gaps concern additional local-first training seeds, the last three HFL-context rows, taxonomy mismatch, and a second private task.
