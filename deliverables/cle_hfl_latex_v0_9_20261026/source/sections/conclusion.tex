\section{Conclusion}

Class--corruption entanglement turns a nominal nuisance into a client-specific class cue in model-heterogeneous federated learning. Source-paired operator interventions and DSA make this response measurable without treating corrupted accuracy as shortcut evidence. The accompanying identification analysis clarifies both what the paired contrast removes and why pseudo-environment improvements cannot certify mitigation on their own. Across three matched training seeds, CLE is predominantly local-first: the Local DSA contrast remains large while the communication difference is small on average and changes sign. This pattern motivates changing the client risk rather than introducing another communication protocol. PEW+BER implements that intervention with an explicit corruption taxonomy and class-conditional support balancing. Completed controlled studies show substantial DSA reductions across bindings and communication bases; the moderate protocol additionally shows a monotone seed-0 dose response and a 51.21\% matched DSA reduction, together with the important qualification that utility is not uniformly preserved. Remaining evidence gaps concern the last three HFL-context rows, taxonomy mismatch, and a second private task.
